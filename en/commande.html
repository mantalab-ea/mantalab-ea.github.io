Frequently asked questions

Is this investment advice?

No. The report describes what the robot did in our tests. Past results do not predict future results.

Are you linked to robot sellers?

No. MantaLab is paid by no seller, nor by MetaQuotes or prop firms.

Why not the seller's report?

Because the seller picks the period, settings and conditions. The lab runs its own tests, in less favourable conditions.

What are the limits?

A test describes the past, on one market and one period. The report always states exactly what was tested.
