Quantum Titan MT5

Tested by the lab on November 25, 2026, version 3.4, on XAUUSD H1, default settings, $10,000 deposit.

High risk

Traps found: fails prop firm rules. On real ticks, the worst equity drawdown goes from 66.0% to 27.4% and the net result from −$6,249 to +$17,942. Risk level: the worse of the two scenarios (high risk with perfect execution, medium risk on real ticks).

How it trades

  • Martingale (nothing to report). No loss-driven size increase detected (increases after a loss: 16%, after a win: 20%).
  • Grid (nothing to report). No adding to losing positions detected.
  • Stop-loss (nothing to report). Positions are opened with a stop-loss.
  • Wins and losses (nothing to report). 72% winning trades; average loss = 2.01× the average win.
  • Prop firm rules (alert). Would fail FTMO 2-Step-type rules (5% daily, 10% total): worst day −7.2%, worst equity drawdown 27.4%.
